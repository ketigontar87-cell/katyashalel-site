This $350 one-time package covers one person, one service, one target market and language, and up to five public website pages. It includes the documented review, five prioritized fixes, three ready-to-use copy blocks and one round of written revisions.

What does ongoing work involve?

Ongoing monitoring, website changes, publishing and retesting after changes are outside this package. Any further work needs a separate agreed scope.

Can you guarantee that AI will recommend us?

No. AI responses vary. Recommendations and sales aren’t guaranteed. The report separates observations from hypotheses and records failed or unusable responses.

How will we know whether a change helped?

Retesting after changes is outside this package. If agreed separately, it requires comparable scenarios and documented conditions; a changed answer alone does not establish what caused it.
