### Method
H2: I do not guess what AI thinks. I run the comparison.

1. Measure
Run controlled prompts across genuine consumer AI surfaces and preserve the raw answers.

2. Diagnose
Separate recognition, retrieval, evidence, corroboration and selection failures instead of collapsing them into one visibility score.

3. Intervene
Change only the parts of the public evidence environment the diagnosis actually implicates.

4. Measure again
Repeat matched conditions and test whether the target moved while matched controls stayed stable.

Trust note: No guaranteed rankings. No invented AI visibility percentage. No claim of causation from one changed answer.

## FAQ production answers

### Why does ChatGPT recommend competitors and not us?
Because knowing that your company exists is different from selecting it for a specific request. The system may retrieve stronger evidence for a competitor, categorize you differently, or infer that another option better fits the user's constraints. The first step is to observe the actual comparison rather than guess.

### Can I influence what ChatGPT says about my company?
You can improve the public evidence environment that AI systems read, but you cannot legitimately control a model's answer. The useful work is to diagnose what is wrong, change the relevant evidence, and measure again under comparable conditions.

### How do I know whether my company is visible in AI answers?
Do not reduce it to one visibility score. Test whether the system can find you, describe you correctly, corroborate important claims and select you under relevant decision conditions.

### Is AI legibility the same as GEO or AI SEO?
No. GEO and AI SEO usually focus on discoverability or citation. AI legibility includes those layers but continues into understanding, corroboration, comparison and selection.

### Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend me?
No. Nobody can credibly guarantee a consumer AI recommendation. What can be guaranteed is a controlled process: measure, diagnose, intervene and verify.

### Does Wikipedia matter?
It can matter, but it is not a universal prerequisite. What matters is whether a system can retrieve enough consistent, independent evidence to resolve who you are and what claims about you are defensible.

### Does structured data matter?
Sometimes. Structured data can improve machine readability and entity resolution, but it does not create independent authority by itself.

### Do reviews and press coverage matter?
They can, when they provide relevant independent evidence. Their value depends on what claim needs corroboration and whether the source is actually retrievable by the system.

### Why do ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity give different answers?
They use different models, retrieval systems, product rules, context and update cycles. That is why a serious baseline preserves results by surface instead of averaging them into one answer.

### Can AI know my company and still categorize it incorrectly?
Yes. Recognition can be correct while category assignment is wrong or too broad. That can change which comparisons and recommendations you enter.

### How long does it take for a change to show up?
There is no universal propagation time. Different systems retrieve, index and refresh on different schedules. That is why T1 measurement is dated and surface-specific.

### How do you separate a real improvement from model drift?
Repeat matched prompts, preserve raw outputs and inspect matched control questions. If the target changes while controls remain stable, the intervention hypothesis becomes stronger. It is still not automatic proof of causation.
