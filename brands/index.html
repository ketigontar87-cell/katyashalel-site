Brand Legibility Diagnostic

See how AI compares your company, and what is worth changing

I work with founders and marketing teams who need to understand what AI tells potential customers about their company, products or expertise. We start with the buying decisions that matter to your business and investigate the answers people could receive.

When this work is useful

  • ChatGPT recommends competitors when someone asks about your category.
  • AI describes your company incorrectly, uses outdated facts or confuses the founder with another business.
  • You have visibility reports but still do not know what to change.
  • You need to decide whether a one-time audit, specialist help or ongoing monitoring is worth buying.

What you receive

  • An agreed set of customer decision scenarios, with the subject, market, language and constraints kept explicit.
  • A dated record of the questions, full answers and observable sources, including failed or unusable attempts.
  • A comparison of how you and the alternatives are described, shortlisted and recommended in those answers.
  • A diagnosis that separates factual errors, evidence gaps and plausible explanations from what remains unknown.
  • A prioritized plan that says what to change, why it is worth considering and how the change could be checked.

Where my judgment comes in

I review the business context, the distinctions between a company, its products and its founder, and the decisions your customers actually need to make. I connect the observations to changes your team can evaluate and carry out. A model's explanation is one piece of evidence to investigate, not access to its internal reasoning.

Choosing the right scope

When is a one-time audit enough?

The scope should follow the decision at hand. A focused audit can help clarify a specific problem, prepare for a launch or establish whether further work is worthwhile. Implementation and a follow-up test need an agreed scope. Further checks make sense when important scenarios continue to matter and the team is ready to act on the findings.

What does ongoing work involve?

Checking whether important changes have held, investigating meaningful shifts and deciding what to do next. That can include correcting outdated facts, reviewing a changed source or competitor, and testing an approved intervention. The frequency should reflect the stakes and pace of change. Rewriting content every month is not the goal.

Can you guarantee that AI will recommend us?

No. We can define relevant tests, document what appears, improve evidence and access, and check the result. We cannot control every model, source or personalized answer. A recommendation in a test is also different from an inquiry or a sale; business outcomes need their own evidence.

How will we know whether a change helped?

If you commission a follow-up test, we compare repeated observations using the same core scenarios and closely matched conditions, with a change log and failed attempts retained. Implementation and follow-up testing are separate from the initial diagnostic. A changed answer alone does not establish which change caused it.
