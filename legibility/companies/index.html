AI legibility · For companies

AI systems now shape the shortlist. Legibility determines whether they can understand, evaluate and seriously consider you.

No one can guarantee you a ChatGPT recommendation, and anyone who does is selling noise. What can be measured is how machines understand, consider and select your company, and whether that changes after an intervention. That is what I sell: controlled diagnosis, directed interventions, and controlled verification, with a published protocol behind the word controlled.

What changed

The selections you never see

Your next customer asks ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity or Gemini before they ever reach your site. The model assembles a picture of your company from whatever evidence it can read, decides whether you belong in the consideration set, and recommends someone. This happens across thousands of buying conversations nobody on your team ever sees.

Companies lose these selections for reasons unrelated to product quality: the models hold a thin or outdated picture of the business, misread the product line, or can't corroborate its claims because the only source is the company's own website. I call that last one the corroboration gap, and it can be one reason a company is mentioned but not selected. And because the environment itself keeps changing, this is a managed function, not a one-time fix.

The engagement

Diagnose. Intervene. Steward.

One decision cycle: measure, explain, decide, your teams execute, verify, adapt. I own the question of how machines understand and select your company. Your teams keep their jobs; I am not your agency.

01
Diagnose
4 to 6 weeks

Legibility Sprint

A reconstruction of how AI systems currently perceive your company: what the models believe you are, who they think you compete with, where you enter consideration sets and where you lose the selection, which claims they can corroborate and from which sources, and where the models disagree. Then the same depth on your public evidence layer: site, structured data, product information, press, reviews, third-party sources.

  • Frozen prompt sets across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini, with repeated independent runs, recorded retrieval conditions and control conditions
  • A testable diagnosis of what is likely driving selection loss
  • Intervention plan ranked by expected leverage and evidentiary confidence, not by effort
  • Delivered as a decision record and an executive presentation
02
Intervene

Interventions

Your SEO, PR, content, product and engineering teams execute. I direct the interventions: requirements go to teams as specifications, and every material decision is accepted, rejected or redirected against the legibility architecture. I don't do their work, and that boundary is deliberate.

  • Specifications, not tasks: entity structure, explicit claims, evidence architecture, product taxonomy
  • PR briefs stated as corroboration targets: which claims need independent confirmation, not impressions
  • One senior owner on your side, a regular decision cycle, no standing meetings with execution teams
03
Steward
6 to 12 months

Legibility Stewardship

The environment keeps moving after the program ships. When something shifts, a source class collapses, a new shopping layer appears, retrieval changes, the question is never "react to everything." It is: does this materially change your evidence architecture or your selection performance? If no, we do nothing. If yes, we reallocate. That judgment is what the retainer buys.

  • Continuous controlled measurement against the Sprint baseline, quarterly deep audits
  • Competitive monitoring: who is displacing you in consideration sets, and on what evidence
  • A running decision record, so every intervention and every retracted hypothesis stays on the books
  • Limited seats. This is a senior strategic function, not a scalable agency service

Why this is not another AI visibility vendor

The control condition

Every AI visibility vendor can show you a dashboard going up. Almost none can tell you whether the movement is yours. Models change behavior constantly, and when they do, scores move for everyone at once.

Synthetic Term Control is a negative-control protocol I published as open research. Alongside your real measurements, we track control terms that should not respond to your interventions. When your metrics move while the controls stay stable, we have materially stronger evidence that the change is specific to the work rather than a system-wide shift. When the client metrics and the controls move together, that is evidence against attributing the movement to the intervention alone, and you should not be billed a success fee for it.

Share of Model

A measurement framework, not a score

Share of Model is the successor to share of shelf: the fraction of machine-run selections your company can participate in. It is deliberately not a single percentage, because single scores in a moving environment become vanity metrics. It is a framework of six measured layers, frozen at the Sprint as a baseline and re-run under the same conditions on a set cadence. A quarter later the conversation is not "why doesn't ChatGPT recommend us yet" but "here is what changed in machine perception, and here is the next decision."

  • Entity understandingDo models know what your company is, what it makes, and how its parts connect
  • Consideration presenceIn which buying questions you appear as an option at all
  • Recommendation presenceWhere models actively select you
  • Competitive displacementWho takes the selection instead of you, and on what evidence
  • Claim corroborationWhich of your claims models can support from sources you don't own
  • Cross-model consistencyWhether ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini tell the same story about you
Guarantees

What I guarantee, and what nobody can

Nobody can guarantee

  • That a specific model will recommend you
  • Rankings inside AI answers
  • That a given source will be cited
  • That today's tactics survive the next retrieval change

I guarantee

  • A documented, repeatable measurement protocol using frozen prompt sets, repeated runs and control conditions
  • A testable diagnosis of what is likely driving selection loss
  • Interventions prioritized by expected leverage, evidentiary confidence and implementation cost
  • Verification of whether measurable change occurred, and an honest record when a hypothesis fails and what replaces it

SEO never guaranteed the first position on Google, and PR never guaranteed the FT. What separates a discipline from a pitch is whether the evidence chain is explicit: observed machine behavior, diagnosis, intervention, repeated measurement, and a record of what changed, including when the hypothesis failed.
