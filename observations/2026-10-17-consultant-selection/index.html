The specialist you're looking for is an AI visibility auditor or Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) consultant—ideally someone experienced with individual experts, independent consultants, and professional services rather than just large brands.

Your question is more sophisticated than traditional SEO. You're trying to understand:

AI's perception of your expertise: How ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity describe your qualifications, specializations, and credibility.

Competitive recommendations: Which other consultants AI recommends for work you're qualified to do, and why.

Missing or misleading information: Whether AI misunderstands your experience, overlooks important achievements, or associates you with the wrong specialties.

Sources of influence: Which websites, publications, professional profiles, and third-party references appear to support its recommendations.

Corrective action: What evidence and positioning changes could improve the accuracy of your representation and your likelihood of being recommended.

I would begin with a one-time AI reputation and competitive visibility audit, rather than sign up immediately for ongoing SEO services.

What I would require from an investigator

Ask for a written report containing:

A record of what several AI systems actually say about you.

A comparison against five to ten relevant specialists.

An assessment of which parts of your expertise are correctly represented, missing, or inaccurate.

An analysis of cited evidence and plausible reasons competitors appear more often.

Prioritized recommendations for improving your professional positioning.

The investigator should repeat tests because AI recommendations vary between sessions and over time. They should also distinguish observable evidence from speculation about how a model internally makes decisions. No outside consultant can reliably guarantee an AI recommendation.
