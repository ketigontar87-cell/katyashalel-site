## AI Legibility

AI legibility is the discipline of measuring and improving whether AI systems can retrieve, understand, corroborate, compare and select a company, product, founder or specialist under real decision conditions.

Selection and recommendation are observed outcomes, not guaranteed results.

The measured diagnostic chain used in current research is:

Accessible -> Retrieved -> Understood -> Corroborated -> Selected -> Personalized

This is a diagnostic framework. It is not a claim that a model exposes these as internal stages. “Trust” is not treated as a directly measurable model state.

## Relationship to adjacent fields
AI legibility sits across AI Search and GEO, technical SEO, digital PR, entity and brand authority, and measurement.

- AI Search / GEO / AEO: discovery, citation and answer visibility.
- Technical SEO: crawlability, rendering, canonicalization, internal links, structured data and information architecture.
- Digital PR: independent external evidence and coverage.
- Entity and brand authority: identity resolution, disambiguation, credible external references and coherent associations.
- Measurement: controlled observation of retrieval, description, comparison and selection under stated conditions.

These are inputs and adjacent disciplines, not synonyms for AI legibility. One ChatGPT answer is one observation, not market proof.

## Method

Measure → Diagnose → Fix → Measure again.

Measurement uses frozen prompt sets, repeated clean-session runs, recorded retrieval/search state, raw outputs and surface-local metrics where comparable. Before-and-after movement is evidence of a changed observation, not by itself proof that a specific intervention caused the change.

## The recommendation boundary

Canonical explanation: https://katyashalel.com/why-ai-knows-but-doesnt-recommend/

An AI system can identify a company correctly yet omit it from a buyer-intent answer when the retrieved evidence is weak, contradictory, poorly matched to the user's constraints, or less defensible than the evidence available for alternatives.

Useful distinction:

Known -> the system can resolve the entity.
Understood -> it can describe the entity without guessing.
Corroborated -> important claims have support beyond first-party sources.
Selected -> the entity is chosen for a specified prompt and constraints.

Being cited does not guarantee being recommended. Being known does not guarantee being selected.

## Boundaries

- No practitioner can guarantee a position, citation, recommendation or selection inside an AI system.
- Model outputs can differ by product surface, model version, retrieval mode, language, market, personalization and stochastic sampling.
- A before-and-after change does not by itself establish causality.
- Model rationale text is not direct evidence of an internal trust state.
- A first-party claim does not become independent corroboration because it is repeated across more first-party pages.
- Sponsored distribution is an intervention. It is not independent evidence by definition.
- Claims scoped to a market, language, surface or test set must keep that scope when quoted.
