How I approach it

Start with a buying situation that matters to the business. Preserve the answer and its visible sources. Record a mention, a shortlist appearance and an explicit recommendation separately.

Then compare what AI says with the actual offer and available evidence. Separate factual errors from possible explanations, and identify changes worth testing.

The output should make the next step clear: which claim to clarify, which page to update, what evidence to add and what to check again. That is the workflow we’re building into Legibi.

Why that matters commercially

Every change competes for your team’s time and budget. Before commissioning more content or another website rewrite, you need a reason to believe that work addresses the problem.

A documented review gives you something concrete to evaluate and act on. A follow-up check can show what changed. Recommendations, enquiries and sales remain separate outcomes to measure.
