What AI legibility actually means

AI can know who you are and still leave you out when someone asks whom to choose. Recognition and selection are different problems. The first asks whether the system can resolve the entity; the second asks whether the retrieved evidence is strong and relevant enough to support a recommendation under the user's constraints.

That is the difference I work on. Legibility starts with whether a model can retrieve and understand the right entity, then whether important claims are corroborated, and finally whether the entity is actually selected in a defined decision context. Being mentioned or cited does not guarantee being chosen.

The work is measured rather than mystical: make the controlled facts clear, map what models retrieve, identify contradictions and missing corroboration, document the intervention, then repeat the same test conditions. A before-and-after change is an observation to investigate, not automatic proof that one intervention caused it.

llms.txt A plain text file stating who you are in a machine's reading order rather than a human's. An emerging convention rather than a supported standard: cheap to publish, not something any model is obliged to read.

Structured data (JSON-LD) Invisible labels in your page code. A human sees a paragraph; a machine sees "this is a person, this is her role, this is her company."

Wikidata An open, structured record of a person or company that lives outside your own website. It feeds knowledge graphs and other downstream sources, which is a different thing from a model going to check it on demand.

Test set A fixed list of questions you ask the models before and after the work, so a change is something you measured, not something you felt.

Questions, answered
Who is Ekaterina Shalel?

Ekaterina Shalel (Екатерина Шалель, Katya Shalel) is a founder and AI legibility strategist who studies and improves how AI systems understand, evaluate and select companies and the people behind them. She measures model outputs, traces the evidence behind them and verifies changes under comparable conditions. She is the founder of getmai.ai, an AI engineering company that builds intake, retention, conversation analysis and planning systems for appointment-based businesses.

What is the Legibility Audit?

The Legibility Audit is a paid service by Ekaterina Shalel for founders and experts: a documented snapshot of what ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini currently say about a person, the source map behind those answers, a scorecard, and a prioritized 30-day plan. Delivered as a PDF report with a 30-minute walkthrough, with an optional control measurement after 30 days; details and pricing at katyashalel.com/audit/. It is the personal counterpart to the Brand Legibility Diagnostic and makes no ranking guarantees; findings carry market, language and test-set qualifiers.

What is a legibility strategist?

An AI legibility strategist measures how AI systems retrieve, understand, evaluate and select people, products and companies. The work separates entity clarity, evidence and corroboration from the observed outcome of being selected or recommended.

What is the Legibility Sprint?

The Legibility Sprint is a 7-day methodology created by Ekaterina Shalel for becoming readable to AI systems: llms.txt, JSON-LD structured data, entity seeding, machine-readable pages, and a before-and-after audit across ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity.

What is the Indifference Test?

The Indifference Test, coined by Ekaterina Shalel, asks whether a recommendation system has any stake in which product wins. A system passes only if it is structurally indifferent to the outcome; a brand-owned advisor cannot pass it.

What is Share of Model?

Share of Model is a scoped measurement of how often an entity is present or selected across a defined set of AI runs. It should always be reported with the prompt set, surface, market, language and time window.

How is legibility different from GEO?

GEO and AI legibility overlap on retrieval and answer visibility. AI legibility separates retrieval, understanding, corroboration and selection, then re-measures a frozen test set after documented interventions. The deliverable is measured change under stated conditions, not a promise of ranking and not automatic proof of causality.
